Practical Info

  • 🗓
    Dates & times Pick your date and session directly in the ticket selector.
  • ⏱
    Duration Approx. 60–90 minutes (the walking trail itself takes about 45–75 minutes).
  • 📍
    Location Hedley Jorgensen Park, Crescent Rd, Kalamunda WA 6076, Australia.
  • 🅿
    Parking Offsite parking at S&R Orchard with a free shuttle included; limited accessible parking is available onsite (see FAQs).
  • 👨‍👩‍👧
    Age requirement All ages welcome — guests under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.
  • ♿
    Accessibility This is a bushland trail — most of the path is not a hard surface. See the FAQs for full accessibility details.

About the experience

A nighttime walk along a lit outdoor trail with magical creatures, interactive spell moments, sound and lighting design inspired by the Forbidden Forest.

Hedley Jorgensen Park, Crescent Rd, Kalamunda WA 6076, Australia.

The experience is outdoors. If weather forces a cancellation you'll be notified by email at the address linked to your booking.

Most guests spend around 60–90 minutes on site.

Yes — food and drink are available on site, with vegan and gluten-free options.

Regular parking is at S&R Orchard (about a 7-minute drive from the venue) with a free shuttle to the site.

Nothing formal — dressing up is encouraged but not required. Wear something suited to a woodland walk after dark.

It's a family experience, though some moments may feel a little spooky. If anyone in your group has arachnophobia, please contact the team ahead of your visit.

Tickets & access

Tickets are sold via the Fever app (Google Play / App Store) and on the Fever website.

There is a box office on site, but sessions can sell out. Booking in advance is strongly recommended.

No minimum age, but since this is a lit trail at night some younger guests may find parts intense. Guests under 16 must be with an adult.

Yes — motorised scooters are available for hire at the trail entrance. It's a woodland path, so most of the surface is not hard. Guests requiring assistance can apply for one free carer pass.
